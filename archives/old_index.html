About

Designing at every scale, from hospitals to high‑rises.

Keith Traywick is an experienced architect with a career spanning landmark projects across the United States, the Middle East, and Europe. His expertise encompasses design, international project management, urban planning, multi-family residential, healthcare, retail, and high-rise construction — with a particular talent for navigating the complexity of large-scale construction administration.
